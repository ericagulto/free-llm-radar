# Referral programs — what's real, what pays, and what you must disclose

Verified 29 September 2026. Read this before publishing any referral link.

> **Update, 29 Sep:** WorkBuddy and MiniMax have moved from `unknown` to verified. WorkBuddy is
> **live and pays on free usage** — but its window closes **30 September 2026**. MiniMax is
> **paid-only**. Only B.AI remains unverified. See the per-program sections below.

---

## The three problems, up front

**1. Most LLM referral programs only pay on *paid* conversions.**

Z.ai requires the referred user to complete a **paid subscription** — and you need **3 paying
referrals** before anything is released. Accrued rewards sit in a pending state until then.

A free-API directory attracts people who specifically do not want to pay. The funnel and the
payout condition point in opposite directions. This is a structural mismatch, not a tuning problem.

**Two programs are the exception** — they pay without the referred user ever buying anything:

- **QoderWork CN** pays when the referred user consumes 1 Credit on a free plan.
- **WorkBuddy** pays when the referred user simply *uses* the product — 50 points on first use, plus
  100 more if they use it on 3 days within a week.

Both are genuinely compatible with a free-tier audience. Everything else on this page is not.

**2. Qoder's international referral program is dead.**

Its own terms state the invite programme **closed 30 January 2026**, and that historical
invitations can no longer earn Credits. Multiple blogs still list it as active. They are stale —
the same class of error as the "GitHub Models is free" listings.

**3. Disclosure is legally required, and the ranking-influence disclosure specifically applies to you.**

The FTC treats referral rewards as a material connection **even when the reward is non-monetary
credits**. Penalties run to **$51,744 per violation**, and each undisclosed link can count
separately. California, New York and Illinois run their own stacked enforcement.

The FTC has specifically prosecuted the pattern this project would fall into:
- a national review site with undisclosed commissions influencing its "Best of" rankings
- a travel comparison site with CPA commissions behind "unbiased" rankings

A ranked directory of free API providers with referral links **is** that pattern. The disclosure
has to be prominent, not tucked away.

---

## What the disclosure must satisfy

Four tests, all mandatory:

| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Proximity** | Near the link, not separated by paragraphs or buried at the page bottom |
| **Prominence** | At least as large as surrounding text, contrasting colour, never in footnotes or collapsed sections |
| **Unavoidability** | Visible *before or at the same time* as the link — not after a click |
| **Understandability** | Plain language. "Partner links" and "may contain sponsored content" both fail |

**Explicitly non-compliant:**
- Disclosure in the page footer only
- A separate "Disclosure Policy" page linked from the footer
- A collapsed "read more" section
- Small grey text in a wall of same-colour text

A single top-of-page disclosure **can** cover multiple links if it is persistent while scrolling
(a sticky banner qualifies). If the page mixes referral and non-referral sections, add a separate
disclosure near the referral section.

The dashboard ships with a sticky disclosure banner above the results. Do not remove it.

## Program-by-program

### QoderWork CN — live, and the only good fit

| | |
|---|---|
| **Reward** | 200 Credits per successful referral, +1,000 per 5 |
| **Cap** | 200 referrals / 40,000 Credits lifetime |
| **Trigger** | Referred user registers, logs into the client, and consumes ≥1 Credit within 14 days |
| **Paid required?** | **No** — works on a free plan |
| **Get your link** | https://qoder.com.cn/referral |
| **Terms** | https://help.aliyun.com/zh/lingma/qoderwork-cn-new-user-credits-claim-and-referral-reward-program-terms-and-conditions |

**What the terms prohibit:** spam, unsolicited messages, harassment, and "fraudulent, unethical or
coercive means of obtaining referrals." Also self-referral, fake accounts, and "spam or other
malicious promotion methods."

**What this means for you:** a public directory is defensible. Mass-posting the link into forums,
comment sections or group chats is not — that is the behaviour the clause targets. The terms also
require compliance with advertising law and truthful representation, and prohibit disparaging the
provider. All of which the disclosure banner and an honest listing already satisfy.

Credits expire **1 month** from issuance. Referred-user status updates periodically, so there is
a lag between a referral completing and the reward landing.

### Z.ai / GLM — live, but paid-only

| | |
|---|---|
| **Reward** | 10% of the referred user's first order value, in Credits |
| **Bonus** | +10% of the total for every cumulative 30 valid referrals |
| **Cap** | No upper limit on invites |
| **Trigger** | Referred user completes their **first paid subscription within 72 hours** of registering |
| **Paid required?** | **Yes** — and 3 paying referrals are needed before payout begins |
| **Get your link** | https://z.ai/subscribe?invitedialog=true |
| **Terms** | https://docs.z.ai/devpack/credit-campaign-rules |

**The 3-person threshold is the catch.** Until three referred users have paid, everything accrued
stays pending and unusable. For a free-tier audience, that threshold may never be reached.

**What the terms prohibit:** "artificial engagement" — explicitly including *paying for services,
joining booster groups, or exchanging non-genuine social engagement to complete tasks.* Fraud
detection runs across phone number, email, device ID (IMEI/OAID/IMSI), IP address and payment
account. Associated accounts are disqualified as a group and rewards reclaimed.

Credits are non-transferable, non-refundable, non-giftable. They are long-term valid, though the
platform reserves the right to change that with 30 days' notice.

### Qoder (international) — DEAD

The terms page states the programme ended **30 January 2026** and that historical invitations can
no longer earn Credits. Do not list this as available.

### WorkBuddy (Tencent) — live, and pays on free usage

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inviter reward** | **50 points** when the referred user first uses the product |
| **Inviter activity bonus** | **+100 points** if the referred user uses it on 3 days within 7 days |
| **Referred user gets** | **2,000 points** on signup (new-user benefit) |
| **Both sides** | **+500 points each** if the referred user pays for a personal upgrade within 30 days |
| **Trigger** | Registration **plus actual use** — registering alone does not count |
| **Paid required?** | **No** for the 50 + 100. Yes for the 500 bonus |
| **Window** | **即日起至 30 September 2026** |
| **Get your link** | https://www.workbuddy.cn/events/invite/ |
| **Terms** | Same page — the rules are inline, not on a separate terms URL |

**Why this is the best fit on the list.** The referred user gets 2,000 points for signing up, which
makes the link genuinely worth sending rather than a favour you are asking. And you are paid on
their *free* usage, so the funnel and the payout condition finally point the same way. Realistic
yield is 150 points per active referral without any purchase.

**The catch is the window.** The event page states the program runs to **30 September 2026**. If you
are reading this after that date, re-check before sharing — Tencent has already run at least one
prior version of this campaign (see below).

**Do not cite the older campaign.** `copilot.tencent.com/fission/` still serves a page describing a
"合伙养虾计划" with different numbers — 100 Credits per valid invite, +300 per 20, 3,000 Credits for
the invitee, +1,000 each on paid upgrade, deadline 30 April. That page is marked **活动结束
(ended)**. Search results and blog posts still surface it. The live program is the `workbuddy.cn`
one above, with different numbers.

**Restrictions:** WorkBuddy reserves the right to change the rules with prior notice, and to cancel
rewards and freeze accounts on violation. The paid bonus makes the corresponding order
non-refundable and already-credited points are not clawed back. Points are platform credits that
offset your own usage — not cash, not withdrawable.

### MiniMax — live, but paid-only

| | |
|---|---|
| **Inviter reward** | Credits equal to **10% of what the referred user actually pays** |
| **Referred user gets** | **10% off** Token Plan at checkout, on all plans and upgrades, repeatable |
| **Trigger** | Referred user completes an **eligible paid order** |
| **Paid required?** | **Yes** |
| **Credits expire** | 90 days from issue |
| **Get your link** | https://platform.minimax.io/subscribe/coding-plan |
| **Terms** | https://platform.minimax.io/docs/token-plan/faq |

**Verified from MiniMax's own Token Plan FAQ**, which is the authoritative source — third-party
blogs quote several different versions of this program with different end dates, all stale.

Your credits can **only** offset MiniMax Open Platform API fees. They cannot be withdrawn,
transferred, or converted. A fraudulent refund on the referred order revokes the reward.

The shareable half is the invitee discount. If you are writing a listing aimed at people who want
free access, MiniMax's own referral program gives them nothing free — it gives them 10% off
something they would have to buy.

### B.AI — still unverified

A documented 300,000-Credit invite gift pack, third-party reported. Gift credits expire 30 days
after issuance. **Still not confirmed from a primary source** — B.AI publishes no readable referral
terms page. Do not publish this link until you have read the terms yourself.

## Where to put your links

`referrals.js` → `window.YOUR_LINKS`. Leave a value empty and the dashboard falls back to the
plain signup URL with no referral tracking — the safe default.

```js
window.YOUR_LINKS = {
  qoderwork_cn: 'https://qoder.com.cn/r/YOURCODE',   // paste your own
  zai:          '',
  workbuddy:    '',
  minimax:      '',
  bai:          ''
};
```

You have to generate these yourself from each provider's console. Nobody can generate them for
you, and you should never paste someone else's referral link into your own project — it is their
tracking, and you would be sending your traffic to them.

## Honest assessment

The referral angle is worth pursuing, but not as the primary purpose of this project.

**Where it works:** a genuinely useful directory that happens to carry disclosed referral links
earns slowly but compounds, and does not put your accounts at risk. QoderWork CN and WorkBuddy are
the two programs whose payout conditions match the audience — both pay on free usage. Neither pays
in cash: both pay in platform credits that offset your own usage of that platform. If the goal is
actual income rather than reduced bills, neither program delivers it, and nothing else on this list
does either.

**Where it fails:** if the directory's real purpose becomes capturing signups, three things go
wrong at once — the FTC exposure becomes real rather than theoretical, providers with
anti-spam clauses may disqualify you, and the listings stop being trustworthy because you now have
a reason to rank referable providers higher.

**The cheap test:** if a provider with no referral program is genuinely the best option for a given
use case, does your directory say so, prominently? If yes, it is a directory. If no, it is an ad
with a table around it — and that is the version that gets prosecuted.

The dashboard is built so both can coexist: referral-linked offers are flagged, filterable, and
excluded from influencing the default sort.
