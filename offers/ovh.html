What this is

OVHcloud's AI Endpoints are free to call anonymously, and unusually they cover transcription and embeddings as well as chat. European hosting is the distinguishing feature.

What it's good for

  • EU data-residency prototyping, where most free tiers are US or China hosted
  • Whisper transcription without a key
  • Embeddings for retrieval prototypes

Caveats and red flags

  • ~2 requests/minute anonymously, which is very slow.
  • No key means shared per-IP limits and no way to raise them.
  • The endpoint hostname is long and easy to mistype.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Limited English-language coverage. OVHcloud is well known in European hosting circles but the AI Endpoints free tier is rarely reviewed.

What we could verify

  • Anonymous tier includes chat, embeddings and Whisper transcription. Source: OVHcloud AI Endpoints documentation
  • Anonymous access is limited to roughly 2 requests/minute. Source: OVHcloud AI Endpoints documentation
Our take opinion

Two things make this stand out: EU hosting, and Whisper on a free tier. Both are uncommon. The 2 req/min ceiling means you will use it for small jobs, but for a European team with a compliance constraint it may be the only option here that qualifies.
