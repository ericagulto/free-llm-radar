Set it up

  1. Join the AMD AI Developer Program at developer.amd.com/ai-developer-program — free, English, 2–3 business days for approval.
  2. Go to Member Perks → Request Cloud Credits and fill in the form.
  3. Choose the Fireworks AI route over the GPU route.
  4. Redeem at app.fireworks.ai/signup.

Test it

curl https://api.fireworks.ai/inference/v1/chat/completions \ -H "Authorization: Bearer $FIREWORKS_API_KEY" \ -H "Content-Type: application/json" \ -d '{"model":"accounts/fireworks/models/llama-v3p3-70b-instruct","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}'
