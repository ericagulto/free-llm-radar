What this is

Shanghai AI Lab's Intern InkStone offers a monthly free allowance during its beta, covering a broad roster of Chinese models including DeepSeek, GLM and Kimi from one console.

What it's good for

  • A renewable floor for Chinese open-weight models
  • Accessing DeepSeek, GLM and Kimi through a single console
  • Long-running side projects that need a monthly reset rather than a countdown

Caveats and red flags

  • Beta — the allowance and the point conversion could change.
  • Measured in "ink points" whose token equivalence varies by model, so a token figure is model-dependent.
  • China-based hosting and a Chinese-language console.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Little English-language coverage. Chinese-language discussion focuses on model quality rather than the free allowance.

What we could verify

  • One ink point offsets up to roughly 50M tokens, measured on DeepSeek-V4-Flash. Source: Intern InkStone console
  • Free monthly allowance during the beta period. Source: Intern InkStone console
Our take opinion

The monthly refresh is what makes this worth having. Most one-off grants here are spent once and gone; a tier that resets gives you a reliable floor for side projects. The point-based metering means you should not expect a predictable token figure.
