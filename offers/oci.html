What this is

Oracle's $300 cloud credit, valid 30 days — the same headline figure as Vertex with a third of the time.

What it's good for

  • A focused 30-day evaluation of OCI Generative AI
  • Short projects where you already know what you are building

Caveats and red flags

  • 30 days is the shortest window of the major cloud grants.
  • OCI signature auth is noticeably more work to set up than a Bearer token.
  • A card is required.
  • Only claim it when you have a plan ready to execute.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Limited coverage of OCI Generative AI specifically. Oracle's broader free tier is better documented than its AI services.

What we could verify

  • $300 credit valid for 30 days, requiring a payment method. Source: Oracle Cloud Free Tier terms
  • Authentication uses OCI request signing rather than a Bearer token. Source: OCI Generative AI documentation
Our take opinion

The 30-day window is the problem. A $300 credit you have to spend in a month is a deadline, not an opportunity — unless you already know exactly what you are building, you will spend the first week reading documentation and the last week panicking.
