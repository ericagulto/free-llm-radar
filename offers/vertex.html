What this is

Google Cloud's $300 credit, valid 90 days, usable across Vertex AI and Model Garden including Gemini. The largest absolute credit here, attached to the platform most likely to produce a surprise bill.

What it's good for

  • Production-shaped prototyping on Google's stack
  • Model Garden access to a wide range of third-party models
  • Anything you might later migrate to Vertex

Caveats and red flags

  • Converts to paid silently when the credit expires or runs dry. Set the budget alert at activation, not later.
  • A card is required.
  • Service-account JSON auth is more setup than a Bearer token.
  • 90 days is longer than OCI or Azure but shorter than Bedrock.

Reviews and reputation

Some independent coverage Google Cloud's free trial is extremely widely covered, usually in the context of billing surprises rather than AI specifically.

What we could verify

  • $300 credit valid for 90 days, requiring a payment method. Source: Google Cloud free trial terms
  • Auth uses a service-account JSON key rather than a Bearer token. Source: Vertex AI documentation
Our take opinion

The biggest number on the credit list and the one I would be most careful with. The failure mode is well documented: the credit ends, the billing starts, and nobody notices until the invoice. If you claim it, create the budget alert in the same session.
