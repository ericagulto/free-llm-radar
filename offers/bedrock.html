What this is

Amazon's grant of up to $200 in Bedrock credits, valid six months — by some distance the most forgiving window among the major clouds.

What it's good for

  • Claude, Llama, Nova and Titan through a single API
  • Long-running evaluations that will not finish in a month
  • Anything where you want to defer the decision about which model to commit to

Caveats and red flags

  • AWS SigV4 request signing — you cannot simply swap a base URL, you need the AWS SDK.
  • A card is required.
  • The credit amount is "up to" $200 rather than a flat figure.
  • Bedrock model availability varies by region.

Reviews and reputation

Some independent coverage Bedrock is well covered as a service. The credit programme specifically is less documented, and the "up to" qualifier is often reported as a flat $200.

What we could verify

  • Credit is up to $200 with a six-month validity window. Source: AWS Bedrock documentation
  • Requests are signed with AWS SigV4 rather than a Bearer token, so the AWS SDK is required. Source: AWS Bedrock documentation
Our take opinion

The six-month window makes this the only cloud grant here you can treat as a real evaluation budget rather than a sprint. The SigV4 requirement is the price: you cannot drop it into an OpenAI-compatible client, which rules it out for quick experiments.
