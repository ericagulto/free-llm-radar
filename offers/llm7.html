What this is

An OpenAI-compatible endpoint where the API key is genuinely optional. Unusual in that anonymous access works rather than being a degraded fallback.

What it's good for

  • Proving your HTTP plumbing works before signing up anywhere
  • Teaching and demos where signup friction would get in the way
  • A last-resort fallback when every key you have is exhausted

Caveats and red flags

  • ~60 requests/hour anonymous, which is enough to test but not to use.
  • No published SLA and a small model roster.
  • Independent operation — capacity and longevity are not guaranteed.

Reviews and reputation

No independent reviews found No meaningful independent review coverage found. Treat any claims about it as unverified.

What we could verify

  • The API key is optional; anonymous requests are accepted. Source: LLM7 documentation
  • Anonymous access is limited to roughly 10 req/min and 60 req/hour. Source: LLM7 documentation
Our take opinion

A genuinely useful utility rather than a provider. When you are debugging why a client will not connect, having an endpoint that needs no key removes a variable. That is the whole value, and it is a real one.
