What this is

A free daily allowance on SambaNova's inference platform, hosting DeepSeek and MiniMax models on custom silicon.

What it's good for

  • An occasional high-capability fallback when other caps are exhausted
  • Evaluating DeepSeek and MiniMax models on non-GPU hardware

Caveats and red flags

  • 20 requests per day is extremely tight — roughly one debugging session.
  • 200K tokens/day sits behind that request cap, so neither limit is generous.
  • Not viable as a primary provider.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Coverage concentrates on SambaNova's hardware and funding rather than the developer free tier, which is rarely discussed.

What we could verify

  • Free tier is 20 requests/day and 200K tokens/day. Source: SambaNova Cloud documentation
  • Serves DeepSeek and MiniMax model families. Source: SambaNova Cloud console
Our take opinion

Twenty requests a day is the number that matters, and it is small enough that this is really a sampler rather than a tier. Worth having configured as a fallback for the one day your primary is rate-limited.
