What this is

StepFun gives away days of its Coding Plan to promote Step 5 Preview, a 600B-parameter sparse mixture-of-experts model with a 1M-token context window. Registration grants 15 days, a first successful call grants another 15, and each invite adds more up to a 45-day bonus ceiling.

What it's good for

  • A few weeks of heavy coding-agent work on a frontier-class model at no cost
  • Long-document and large-repository work — the 1M context window is the selling point
  • Testing an OpenAI-compatible Chinese endpoint without committing to a subscription

Caveats and red flags

  • The daily claim allocation is capped and frequently exhausted, so you may need several attempts across days.
  • It is a Coding Plan grant measured in days, not a token balance — unused days do not bank.
  • Preview status means the model can change or be withdrawn while you are relying on it.
  • Chinese-language registration flow.

Reviews and reputation

Our take opinion

Genuinely useful if you can catch a daily allocation — the context window is the differentiator and the OpenAI-compatible endpoint saves integration work. Treat the day count as a trial, not as a plan you can build on.

Set it up

  1. Register at platform.stepfun.com and sign in.
  2. Claim the free Coding Plan from the Step 5 Preview activity page — this is a daily-quota giveaway, first-come.
  3. Make one successful call — a further 15 days is credited automatically.
  4. Invite friends for up to 45 more days.
  5. Call the API with the OpenAI-compatible base URL below, or use Step Plan inside the client.
