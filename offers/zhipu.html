What this is

Free daily access to Z.ai / Zhipu's Flash models, with a request allowance that looks generous until you read the concurrency limit.

What it's good for

  • Chinese-language tasks where a domestic model performs better
  • Evaluating GLM Flash models before committing to the paid Coding Plan
  • Light interactive chat

Caveats and red flags

  • One concurrent request. You cannot parallelise at all — a single slow call blocks everything behind it.
  • Models rotate and older Flash versions are decommissioned, so hardcoded IDs break.
  • China-based hosting and a Chinese-language console.
  • The binding constraint is concurrency, not volume, which most people misread.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Mostly covered in Chinese-language sources. English coverage of the free Flash tier is sparse and often conflates it with the paid Coding Plan.

What we could verify

  • Free tier allows approximately 1,000 requests/day but only one concurrent request. Source: Z.ai / Zhipu console
  • Older Flash model versions are retired as new ones are released; the console model page is authoritative. Source: Z.ai / Zhipu console
Our take opinion

The concurrency limit is the whole story. A thousand requests a day sounds like plenty until you realise they have to happen one at a time — for any batch or agent workload this is unusable, and for single-stream chat it is fine.
