What this is

ByteDance is giving every Doubao user 30 days of the Standard plan at no cost, on free and paying accounts alike. It is the consumer assistant rather than the Volcano Ark API platform, so the entitlement lives inside the app and there is nothing to integrate.

What it's good for

  • Trying the cloud computer and task mode without paying for them
  • Running several agents in parallel on work you would otherwise do one piece at a time
  • Comparing ByteDance's flagship assistant against Qwen, Kimi and GLM before committing to any of them

Caveats and red flags

  • Client-bound — no API key, and nothing here can be wired into your own stack.
  • Thirty days from claim; the Standard plan reverts to paid when the entitlement lapses.
  • Chinese-language product with a mainland account and payment flow.
  • The 17 October end date comes from reporting of the vendor announcement rather than the vendor's own campaign page, so treat it as approximate.
  • This is the second identical round in a month, which means the terms can shift again without much notice.

Set it up

  1. Download or upgrade the Doubao PC app (or Doubao Work PC). The grant is attached to the desktop client.
  2. Sign in. Both free and paying accounts are eligible — no card, no code, no invitation.
  3. The 30-day Standard entitlement applies automatically. It covers the cloud computer, task mode and multi-Agent parallelism.
  4. Already claimed the previous round? The next 30 days are granted as the current one expires — no action needed.
