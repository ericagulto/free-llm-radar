What this is

Workers AI bundles a free daily inference allowance into Cloudflare's developer platform, metered in "Neurons" — a normalised compute unit — rather than tokens. The model catalogue skews small and open-weight.

What it's good for

  • Running inference at the edge, close to users, inside a Worker
  • Embeddings and image models alongside text in one platform
  • Anything where the deployment story matters as much as the model

Caveats and red flags

  • Neurons are not tokens. The effective allowance depends heavily on which model you pick, so you cannot predict it arithmetically — budget by testing.
  • The free catalogue is mostly small and older models.
  • The account-scoped URL shape makes it fiddlier to wire up than a plain base URL.

Reviews and reputation

Some independent coverage Well covered as a platform. The free allowance is usually mentioned in passing; there is little dedicated analysis of what 10,000 Neurons buys you.

What we could verify

  • 10,000 Neurons/day free, then $0.011 per 1,000 Neurons. Source: Cloudflare Workers AI pricing

Our take opinion

The only offer here that puts inference at the edge with a real deployment story behind it. The Neuron metering is genuinely awkward — it makes cost impossible to reason about up front — but if you are already on Workers it is free capacity you would otherwise not have.

Set it up

  1. Sign up at dash.cloudflare.com — free account, no card.
  2. Sidebar → AI → Workers AI.
  3. Copy your Account ID from the URL or sidebar — you need it in the base URL.
  4. My Profile → API Tokens → Create Token with Workers AI permissions.

Test it

curl https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/accounts/$CF_ACCOUNT_ID/ai/v1/chat/completions \ -H "Authorization: Bearer $CF_API_TOKEN" \ -H "Content-Type: application/json" \ -d '{"model":"@cf/meta/llama-3.3-70b-instruct-fp8-fast","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}'
