Worth knowing
OpenCode has native support for AMD Radeon Cloud. Add your AMD key under Settings → Providers and you get a portable provider inside a good terminal client.

What this is

A terminal coding agent that gives free access to newly launched models, plus a small token allowance for high-mode use.

What it's good for

  • Terminal-first agentic coding
  • Early access to models on their launch day
  • Running AMD Radeon Cloud through a good terminal client — it has native provider support

Caveats and red flags

  • ~100K tokens on high mode is a small allowance.
  • The free models are client-bound.
  • Which models are free changes with each launch.

Reviews and reputation

Some independent coverage Well covered as an open-source terminal agent. The free-model previews are mentioned in release notes but not analysed.

What we could verify

  • Native support for AMD Radeon Cloud as a provider. Source: OpenCode documentation
  • Free access to models on their launch day, with ~100K tokens in high mode. Source: OpenCode documentation
Our take opinion

The AMD integration is the reason to care. As a standalone free tier it is thin, but as a terminal client wired to a portable free key it is one of the better setups available on this list.
