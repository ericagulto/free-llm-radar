What this is

Mistral's free "Experiment" mode gives standing access to a small set of open models. Mistral no longer publishes the free-tier limits, so the practical ceiling is discovered by hitting it.

What it's good for

  • Prototyping where European data residency matters
  • Small open models where you want a fast, cheap baseline
  • Embeddings alongside chat on one key

Caveats and red flags

  • Experiment-mode traffic may be used for training. Treat it like the Gemini free tier.
  • Limits are unpublished, so you cannot forecast usage.
  • The free model roster skews older and smaller than Mistral's headline models.

Reviews and reputation

Some independent coverage Mistral is well covered as a company; the Experiment tier is usually mentioned only in passing and its terms are rarely analysed.

What we could verify

  • Experiment-mode inputs may be used to improve Mistral's models. Source: Mistral AI legal terms
  • Free-tier rate limits are no longer published; the previous figures were ~1 req/sec and 500K tokens/min. Source: Mistral AI documentation
Our take opinion

The confidentiality terms are the deciding factor, exactly as with Gemini — but here you get a much smaller model roster in exchange for the same caveat, which makes it a harder trade to justify. Use it when EU residency is the requirement.
