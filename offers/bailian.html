What this is

Alibaba Bailian's new-user grant is the largest one-time allocation on this list: 70M tokens, plus 1M per model on top, valid for roughly 90 days. It covers Qwen3.8-Max, DeepSeek, GLM and Kimi.

What it's good for

  • A substantial trial of Qwen3.8-Max without a bill
  • Comparing DeepSeek, GLM and Kimi through one Chinese platform
  • A large evaluation budget for a single project

Caveats and red flags

  • Unused quota is wiped at expiry — it does not roll over.
  • Roughly 90 days is the full life of the grant.
  • China-based platform with a Chinese-language console.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Primarily Chinese-language coverage. The grant size is often understated in English round-ups — earlier write-ups described it as around 1M tokens per model.

What we could verify

  • New-user grant is 70M tokens plus 1M per model, valid approximately 90 days. Source: Alibaba Bailian console
  • Unused quota is not rolled over at expiry. Source: Alibaba Bailian console
Our take opinion

The largest one-time grant here, and the one most likely to be wasted by good intentions. Because it expires and does not roll over, the rational move is to spend it deliberately on something you were going to do anyway rather than saving it for a rainy day that will not arrive in 90 days.

  1. Register at bailian.console.aliyun.com with real-name authentication.

  4. Use it inside the 90-day window — unused quota is wiped on expiry.
