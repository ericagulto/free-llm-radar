What this is

OpenCode Zen is a model gateway from the OpenCode project that lists ten models at zero cost for input and output. Unlike the client-bound coding tools here, it issues a key you can point other software at.

What it's good for

  • Wiring free models into any OpenAI-compatible tool, not just OpenCode itself
  • Trying newly launched models the week they appear
  • Long-context work — several of the free models advertise a 1M-token window

Caveats and red flags

  • Billing details are collected at signup even for free models, and auto-reload tops up $20 whenever the balance falls below $5 — disable it unless you mean to pay.
  • Most of the free models train on your prompts. Only Space Bunny and LongCat 2.5 are stated as zero-retention.
  • Stealth and preview models can be delisted without warning; several here already have no vendor guarantee.
  • A free model reaching a paid model's rate would begin charging you silently.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Little independent review material. Most coverage is community forum discussion about individual free models rather than the gateway.

What we could verify

  • Ten models are listed at no cost across input, output and cached read, each described as available for a limited time. Source: OpenCode Zen docs
  • An OpenCode Zen account and API key are required, and billing details are collected at sign-in. Source: OpenCode Zen docs
Our take opinion

The portable key is the reason this is worth listing — most free-model promotions are trapped inside a single client. The auto-reload behaviour is the reason to read the fine print before you sign up.

Set it up

  1. Sign in to OpenCode Zen and add billing details — required to issue a key even for free models.
  2. Copy the Zen API key.
  3. In the OpenCode TUI run /connect, select OpenCode Zen, and paste the key.
  4. Select any free model from the list.

Test it

curl https://opencode.ai/zen/v1/chat/completions \ -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENCODE_ZEN_KEY" \ -H "Content-Type: application/json" \ -d '{"model":"nemotron-3-ultra-free","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}'
