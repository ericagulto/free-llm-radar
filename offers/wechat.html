What this is

WeChat's Mini Program Growth Plan II gives mini-program developers 1 billion tokens plus 100,000 AI images — the largest headline figure on this list by an order of magnitude, and the most narrowly applicable.

What it's good for

  • Adding AI features to a WeChat mini program
  • Chinese-market consumer applications
  • Any project already committed to the WeChat ecosystem

Caveats and red flags

  • Only useful if you have a WeChat mini program. Personal accounts qualify, but there is no other route.
  • The integration is WeChat-specific, so nothing here is portable to another stack.
  • The headline number is not comparable to the token grants elsewhere — it is scoped to mini-program usage.

Our take opinion

The number is enormous and the applicability is tiny. If you are building a WeChat mini program this is the best offer on the list by a wide margin; if you are not, it is worth nothing at all. Worth knowing it exists before you dismiss the whole Chinese ecosystem as expensive.
