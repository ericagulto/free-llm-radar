What this is

A $100 credit on Anyscale's Ray-based platform — the only genuinely distributed-compute offer on this list, aimed at scaling open-source models across a cluster rather than calling a hosted endpoint.

What it's good for

  • Scaling open-source models across multiple nodes with Ray
  • Distributed batch inference and fine-tuning experiments
  • Learning Ray on managed infrastructure

Caveats and red flags

  • The expiry window is not published anywhere — assume it is short and act accordingly.
  • A card is required.
  • Ray is a real learning curve if you have not used it.
  • This is infrastructure, not a model API, so it does not compare directly to the rest of the list.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Anyscale and Ray are covered as developer infrastructure. The credit programme specifically is poorly documented.

What we could verify

  • $100 credit, with no published expiry window. Source: Anyscale console
  • The platform is built on Ray and targets distributed workloads rather than single-endpoint calls. Source: Anyscale documentation
Our take opinion

The only offer here that gives you a cluster rather than an endpoint, which makes it useful for a different job entirely. The unpublished expiry is the thing I would flag hardest — an undisclosed deadline on a credit is worse than a short published one, because you cannot plan.
