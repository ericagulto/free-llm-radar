What this is

NIM provides free hosted access to a large catalogue — 132 models — behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, gated on phone verification rather than a credit card. It is NVIDIA's developer-relations platform, not a commercial service.

What it's good for

  • Trying many open-weight models without multiple signups
  • Access to GLM and Kimi models that are otherwise awkward to reach
  • Benchmarking the same prompt across a wide model range

Caveats and red flags

  • Community reports describe frequent 429 rate-limit responses and occasional unresponsiveness under load.
  • Rate limits are model-specific and around 40 req/min, so behaviour varies unpredictably.
  • Phone verification is required, which is more friction than most offers here.
  • Do not build a production path on it.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Widely mentioned in developer forums, mostly in the context of reliability complaints rather than model quality. Very little formal benchmarking.

What we could verify

  • Access is gated on phone verification rather than a payment card. Source: NVIDIA build portal
  • Rate limits are approximately 40 requests/minute and vary by model. Source: NVIDIA NIM documentation
Our take opinion

The widest catalogue on this list and the least dependable. I would use it to answer "which model should I pay for" and nothing else — the rate-limit reports are consistent enough across developers that I would not put it behind anything user-facing.

Set it up

  1. Create an account at build.nvidia.com. Email verification, then phone verification.
  2. Pick any model, click Get API Key. Starts with nvapi-.
  3. The same key works across the entire catalogue.

Test it

curl https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1/chat/completions \ -H "Authorization: Bearer $NVIDIA_API_KEY" \ -H "Content-Type: application/json" \ -d '{"model":"z-ai/glm-5.2","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}'
