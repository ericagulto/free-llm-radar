What this is

Cerebras offers a $5 signup credit plus a free daily token allowance on wafer-scale hardware. The free daily allowance is real; the $5 is a finite trial attached to it.

What it's good for

  • Very high tokens-per-second generation for short prompts
  • Batch classification and extraction where throughput matters more than context
  • Latency-sensitive demos

Caveats and red flags

  • This is a finite $5 trial, not a recurring free tier. Do not plan around it.
  • 5 requests/minute is genuinely restrictive.
  • A card is required, unlike most offers here.
  • The Developer plan from $10 lifts limits roughly tenfold, which tells you where the free tier sits.

Reviews and reputation

Some independent coverage Cerebras' speed claims are widely covered. The free tier specifically is less examined, and its trial nature is frequently misreported as a permanent allowance.

What we could verify

  • Includes a $5 signup credit alongside the free daily allowance. Source: Cerebras Cloud console
  • Free tier rate limits are approximately 5–10 req/min with a 1M token/day ceiling. Source: Cerebras rate-limit documentation
Our take opinion

The fastest tokens-per-second you will get for free, by a wide margin, and the 5 req/min ceiling makes it a demo tool rather than a service. Note the card requirement — that is unusual for a free tier and worth knowing before you sign up.
