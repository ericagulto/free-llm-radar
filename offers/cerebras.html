What this is

Cerebras offers wafer-scale inference at speeds nothing else on this list approaches. Its free access is a $5 trial credit that expires 30 days after it is granted, and it requires a verified payment method before the API works at all.

What it's good for

  • Very high tokens-per-second generation for short prompts
  • Batch classification and extraction where throughput matters more than context
  • A latency-sensitive demo where nothing slower will do

Caveats and red flags

  • It is a $5 trial, not a free tier. Once the credit is spent or the 30 days lapse, API access stops.
  • A verified payment method is required before Playground or API access activates — unusual for a free tier.
  • The 30-day clock starts at the grant, not at first use.
  • Free-tier limits are tight: roughly 5 requests/minute with a 1M tokens/day ceiling per model.
  • Several third-party lists still describe this as a standing 1M-tokens/day free tier. The vendor does not.

Reviews and reputation

Some independent coverage Cerebras' speed claims are widely covered. The free tier specifically is widely misreported — many trackers still describe the retired daily allowance as if it were current.

What we could verify

  • New accounts receive $5 in free credits after adding a verified payment method, expiring 30 days after the grant. Source: Cerebras Inference change log, 2026-07-16
  • There is no permanently free tier and no per-model always-free allowance. Source: Cerebras rate-limit documentation
Our take opinion

The fastest tokens-per-second you will get for free, by a wide margin — and the fastest thing on this list to run out. Treat it as a one-month evaluation of very fast inference, not as an allowance to build on. If a list tells you it is 1M tokens/day forever, that list is out of date.

Set it up

  1. Sign up at cloud.cerebras.ai. Playground and API stay locked until you add a payment method.
  2. Add a verified payment method. The $5 credit is only granted after this step — unusual for a free tier.
  3. Create an API key. The $5 is now on the clock: it expires 30 days from the grant.
  4. Spend it deliberately. There is no renewal — once the credit is gone or expired, access stops until you buy credits.

Test it
