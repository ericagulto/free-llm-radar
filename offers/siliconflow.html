What this is

SiliconFlow hosts Chinese open-weight models with a free per-minute allowance, and unusually it includes embeddings and rerankers rather than chat alone.

What it's good for

  • RAG prototypes — free embeddings and rerankers are rare on a free tier
  • DeepSeek model access without a separate provider
  • Chinese-language generation and retrieval work

Caveats and red flags

  • Identity verification is required, which is more intrusive than a card check.
  • 30 req/min and 60K tokens/min are modest.
  • China-based hosting.

Reviews and reputation

Thin independent coverage Mostly Chinese-language coverage. English write-ups exist but focus on the paid tiers and pricing rather than the free allowance.

What we could verify

  • Free tier includes the bge-m3 embedding model and rerankers, not just chat completions. Source: SiliconFlow console
  • Identity verification is required to activate the free tier. Source: SiliconFlow console
Our take opinion

The embeddings and reranker coverage is the differentiator — almost every other free tier here is chat-only, which makes building a retrieval prototype annoying. If you are prototyping RAG on a budget, this is the most useful free tier on the list.

Set it up

  1. Sign up at cloud.siliconflow.cn.

  3. Create an API key in the console.

Test it

curl https://api.siliconflow.cn/v1/chat/completions \ -H "Authorization: Bearer $SILICONFLOW_API_KEY" \ -H "Content-Type: application/json" \ -d '{"model":"deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}'
